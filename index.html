Fast, zero-internet file, folder, and clipboard sharing across Windows PCs over Wi-Fi, Mobile Hotspot, or direct Ethernet.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

FromTo operates exclusively over your local area network (LAN, Mobile Hotspot, or direct Ethernet connection). FromTo does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, files, usage analytics, or telemetry on external servers.

All transfers occur directly peer-to-peer between devices on your local network and are saved locally on your device.
